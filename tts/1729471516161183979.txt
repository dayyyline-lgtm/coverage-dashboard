✨Triple Collagen Cream : Skin barrier game ends here!✨
Introducing our 'Triple Collagen Cream' – a powerhouse of hydration and skin barrier reinforcement. With a blend of collagen, niacinamide, and nourishing oils, this cream enhances elasticity, firmness, and resilience.
Elevate your skincare routine and unveil a radiant complexion with 'Triple Collagen Cream' – your go-to solution for a strengthened skin barrier.
Why It's a Must-Have
💧 Ultimate Hydration & Barrier Support :
Enriched with collagen, niacinamide, and nourishing oils, this cream delivers deep hydration while reinforcing your skin's barrier for enhanced resilience and firmness.
🌈 Fast-Acting Formula, Slow to Show Signs of Aging :
Visibly reduces signs of aging in as fast as 2 weeks by addressing three major skin concerns—all in one cream.
Powered by 16 patented ingredients for pores care, elasticity, and after-spots care, Triple Collagen Cream is dermatologically tested to help reduce the signs of aging.
😌 Gentle & Effective for All Skin Types :
Certified free from 15 harmful ingredients* and dermatologically tested, this non-sticky cream is perfect for daily use to achieve a healthy, radiant glow.

HOW TO USE
1. Use at the final step of your skincare routine
2. Scoop out product and apply evenly onto face
3. Spread the product gently across entire face moving from center outwards
4. Gently pat remaining product into skin for better absorption
WHEN TO USE
✔ When you need an all-in-one cream for mature skin.

👉 Mix 1: Combine with Collagen Jelly Cream for a customized skincare boost.
