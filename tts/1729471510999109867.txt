Product description
Zero Pore Pads : winner of the 2026 Cosmopolitan Reader’s Choice Awards - 2026 Best Beauty Products of the Year!
💎 K-Beauty Bestselling Toner Pad 💎 : Simple & Easy Care for smaller-looking pores
Dual textured facial pads infused with Salicylic Acid(BHA) + AHA + patented pore care ingredients to help exfoliate dead skin cells, clear sebum, and minimize the look of pores.
Dimensions: 3.54 x 3.54 x 3.94 inches
Weight : 155g
Why It's a Must-Have
🌀 Gentle Exfoliates and Refines Pores :
Perfect for those concerned about enlarged pores, seeking smoother skin, battling greasy skin from excess sebum, or troubled by blackheads and whiteheads.
🌱 Power of Nature:
Dive deep with White Willow Bark (Natural BHA), Citric Acid (AHA), and soothing botanical extracts. Nature's finest ingredients come together for optimal skin clarity.
💜 Lavender Calmness :
Experience therapeutic relaxation with our pads, merging lavender's soothing essence with deep cleansing benefits.
WHEN TO USE
✔When your skin needs gentle exfoliation
✔When you want to refine and smooth skin texture
✔When you're worried about enlarged pores
✔When excess sebum makes your skin feel greasy
✔When you're concerned about blackheads & whiteheads
Key Ingredients
💙 BHA (Salicylic Acid) :
Oil-soluble exfoliant, penetrates pores to unclog, refine and soothe. Ideal Ingredient against sebum production in the skin pores.
💙 ANTI SEBUM P :
Patented Ingredient specialized for tightening the look of pores.
💙 AHA :
Water-soluble exfoliant, addresses wrinkles, fine lines and rough, crepey texture.
*The above information pertains only to the characteristics of the raw materials.
HOW TO USE
1. After cleansing, with the embossed side, gently swipe across the face from the center outwards
2. With the silky side, gently swipe your face one more time for a final polishing touch
3. Lightly pat to absorb
Pro Tip
👉 1. Use as a mask For areas that need targeted care, place the pads on the desired areas for about 5 minutes, then remove.
👉 2. Keep in the fridge for a refreshing & calming effect.
👉 3. Body care Apply to areas that need smoothing and brightening, such as the chest, shoulders, back, elbows, and knees.
*Safety & Usage Guidelines
 "This product is suitable for most skin types, including sensitive skin, but some users may experience skin irritation or allergic reactions. A patch test is recommended, and if irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a professional. Additionally, this product is cruelty-free and does not involve animal testing. After use, avoid contact with eyes. In case of contact, rinse immediately with water."
