[FAQ] 
Q: Can I use it daily? 
This set is designed to be used as a daily body care routine, but for the Body Peel Shot, we recommend using it around 2 times per week as a focused care. It is especially effective when dead skin cells have built up. 
Although our product has been formulated with gentle ingredients, please keep in mind that over exfoliation is not good for the skin and can cause irritation. 
Q: Can I use the products on the face and vice versa? 
The Body Peel Shot & Body Wash are formulated to be used on the body. Each formula is tailored to the specific skin type and concerns of its intended area. Since facial and body skin differ in texture and needs, please use them as directed for optimal results.
Our toner pad, toner, and jelly mist work well for both face and body.
